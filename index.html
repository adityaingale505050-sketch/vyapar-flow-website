Frequently Asked Questions

Need help understanding how sheets work? Here are answers to common questions.

Is VyaparFlow really free? Are there any limits?

Yes! VyaparFlow is completely free and runs directly on Google Apps Script. There are no monthly subscriptions, server charges, or limits on invoices or items you can add. It uses your personal Google Drive account space.

Is my shop's sales and inventory data safe?

Absolutely. Unlike normal accounting software that uploads your business transaction details to third-party databases, VyaparFlow saves your records 100% inside your own Google Drive. Google secures the backend, and only you have access.

Can I access this offline or from a phone?

Yes. You can edit items and generate transactions on the go using the official Google Sheets app on Android and iOS. For offline setups, you can turn on offline synchronization in your Google Chrome settings.

Does it calculate GST correctly for mixed items?

Yes. In the Inventory tab, you specify a GST slab (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) for every item. When billing, the system automatically pulls the item rate and slab, splits it into CGST & SGST (equal halves), and formats it inside the invoice summary.
